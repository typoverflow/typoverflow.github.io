# Institution logos

Local copies of institution and company marks, used to identify education and employment affiliations.

- Georgia Tech: official vector interlocking GT, https://gtathl.com/build/img/logo2.svg (linked from https://gtathl.com/).
- Nanjing University: official vector shield, https://www.nju.edu.cn/images/footer_logo.svg. White fill changed to purple for the white page background; shield paths preserved, separate Chinese wordmark paths removed, and viewBox fitted to the shield.
- Moonshot AI: vector mark from Lobe Icons, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/master/packages/static-svg/icons/moonshot.svg. MIT license retained in LICENSE-lobe-icons.txt; currentColor resolved to black for use as an image.
- ByteDance: embedded favicon at https://www.bytedance.com/en/
- Harvard University: https://www.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/cropped-logo-branding-compressed.png?w=192
